# Privacy Policy — Heroic Bastion

**Effective date: 27.09.2026**  
**Developer: SztosGamesStudio**  
**Contact: sztosgamesstudio@proton.me**

## 1. Data in the game

Heroic Bastion does not collect or transmit users’ personal data. The game does not require an account, contains no advertisements, and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

## 2. Data stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, unlocked content, settings, and survey reminder preferences locally on your device.

This data is used solely to operate the game and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties. You can delete it by clearing the app’s data in your device settings or uninstalling the game.

## 3. Optional survey

The game may include a link to an optional, anonymous survey that opens in an external browser. The survey collects feedback to help improve the game.

We do not ask for your name, email address, or other identifying information in the survey. Please do not include personal data in free-text responses. The game does not send saved gameplay data or user or device identifiers to the survey.

Anonymous responses may be retained and analysed to support the game’s development. Because responses are not linked to participants’ identities, locating and deleting a specific response may not be possible.

The survey hosting provider may process technical data associated with your use of its website under its own privacy policy, available on that service.

## 4. Contact

If you contact us by email, we will process your email address and message solely to handle your enquiry. We retain this correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the matter. For privacy enquiries or requests to delete correspondence, contact **sztosgamesstudio@proton.me**.

## 5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the game’s features or data processing practices change. The current version will remain available at the same address, with its effective date shown above.
